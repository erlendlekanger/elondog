How a pair pushes its root
01 / 04

Launch a root.

$DOG goes live on pump.fun from your wallet. In the same transaction its creator fee split is locked on chain: most of it pays whoever builds pairs on it.

02 / 04

Someone pairs it.

$SHIB launches with $DOG as its quote. It trades in $DOG, so every buy of $SHIB is a buy of $DOG first.

03 / 04

The root gets locked.

Every $DOG inside $SHIB's curve or pool is $DOG off the market. More pairs, more locked, less to sell.

04 / 04

Builders get paid.

Every ten minutes $DOG's fees are split between its pairs by how much $DOG each keeps locked, and for how long. Paid in SOL.

The math is public.

Each round, a root's fee pot is split between its pairs. A pair's weight is the root it keeps locked, for every second it keeps it there, times a multiplier that rewards staying.

If you build pairs

Get paid twice for one coin.

Your pair's own creator fee is yours, paid in the root. On top of it you are paid part of the root's fees, in SOL, for the root your pair locks.

  • Pick any root, launch from your own wallet
  • Set your own creator fee, 0.01% to 3%
  • Pairs launched anywhere on pump.fun count too
If you launch a root

Every pair is buy pressure on your coin.

You decide how much of your coin's creator fees fund the pair builders: 50% to 100%. The rest goes straight to your wallet. The split is locked at launch.

  • Each pair is a new market that buys your coin
  • Locked supply sits in curves and pools, not in wallets
  • Fee split locked on chain, admin revoked
