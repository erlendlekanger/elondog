How to play.

LEVEL 01

Spawn a pair

Launch a coin on pump.fun with PVE as its quote, from your own wallet. Pick its name, its picture and its own creator fee, paid to you in PVE.

LEVEL 02

Lock PVE

Your coin trades in PVE. Every buy of it buys PVE first and puts it in your curve, then in your PumpSwap pool. That PVE is off the market for as long as it stays.
